Meet the founder

Built through resilience.
Created for shared possibility.

Hello World Studio was founded by Mollen Garikai, a Zimbabwean digital strategist, author, creator and disability inclusion advocate.

Living with cerebral palsy and communication barriers shaped her understanding of accessibility, opportunity and the power of technology. Those challenges did not reduce the vision; they sharpened it.

AI, digital tools and creative systems became practical ways to communicate, build, publish and contribute. Hello World Studio grew from that lived experience into a platform designed to help other people overcome barriers and turn meaningful ideas into visible, lasting work.

“Great ideas should never be limited by circumstance.”
Mission

Transform ideas into meaningful work and lasting impact.
