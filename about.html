Who we are

A community of creators.
One purpose.

We bring together storytelling, publishing, AI education, media, music, digital products and business support so creators and organisations can move from ideas to meaningful impact.

Born from barriers. Built around possibility.

Technology should expand human possibility—not make people feel left behind.

Hello World Studio grew from lived experience: navigating disability, speech barriers, limited resources and creative ambition. AI became a practical accessibility tool for communication, design, education, music and storytelling.

What began as a way to create a clearer voice became a connected ecosystem designed to help more people participate, build confidence and leave a legacy.

Meet the founder

Built from lived experience.
Designed for shared possibility.

Hello World Studio was founded by Mollen Garikai, a Zimbabwean digital strategist, creator, author and disability inclusion advocate. Her experience with cerebral palsy and communication barriers shaped a studio where accessibility is not an afterthought—it is part of the foundation.

“From my world, to your world, to our world.”

The studio combines human judgement with AI-assisted tools to help creators, organisations and communities communicate, publish, learn and preserve the stories that matter.

Meet Shami

An AI presenter created to
expand access and expression.

Shami is the studio’s AI presenter, educator and creative interface. She helps communicate ideas clearly, host learning experiences and support storytelling where speech barriers or production limits might otherwise stand in the way.

She does not replace the founder’s voice. She extends its reach.
