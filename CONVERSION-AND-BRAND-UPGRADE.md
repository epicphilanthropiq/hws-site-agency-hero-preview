# Hello World Studio Conversion & Brand Upgrade

Implemented on the Phase 5 source package.

## Strategic changes
- Clarified the homepage value proposition around outcomes rather than divisions.
- Added a clear Who We Help section and credibility strip without inventing statistics.
- Reframed major page headings around visitor goals.
- Replaced generic calls to action with specific next steps.
- Connected the Marketplace hero and final CTA to the main Beacons Store.
- Preserved existing URLs, navigation, visual system and SEO structure.

## Positioning
Hello World Studio is presented as a creative technology studio that helps people turn ideas into trusted brands, books, content, digital products and lasting impact.
