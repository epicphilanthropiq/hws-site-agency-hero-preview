Founder’s vision

Every life. Every ability. Every story matters.

Hello World Studio was founded by Mollen Garikai to make powerful creative and AI tools more accessible—especially to African creators, people with disabilities, organisations and communities whose stories deserve to be seen, heard and preserved.

The studio brings together publishing, education, media, music and digital innovation so that meaningful ideas can become lasting work.
